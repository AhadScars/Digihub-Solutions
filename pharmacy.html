About this software

DigiHub Pharmacy POS helps chemists manage day-to-day sales without clutter. Track medicines with batch numbers and expiry dates, bill walk-in customers quickly, and print professional tax invoices.

Each pharmacy account only sees its own stock, customers, and sales. Restaurant or hotel logins are blocked in this app.

What you can do

Medicine inventory

Stock with batch, expiry, GST, and low-stock visibility.

Barcode & search billing

Find by name or barcode, build a cart, complete sales fast.

Suppliers & customers

Vendor and customer records next to sales history.

Reports & invoices

Export PDF / CSV for accounts and audits.
