About DigiHub Solutions

Software for counters that never stop

We build focused Windows apps for pharmacies, restaurants, hotels, and schools — practical tools staff can learn the same day.

Our story

DigiHub Solutions started with a simple observation: small and mid-size stores were forced to choose between messy spreadsheets and bloated enterprise suites. Owners needed something in the middle — fast billing, GST-ready invoices, and cloud-backed records — without a full IT department.

We chose separate desktop apps for each trade. A chemist does not need hotel occupancy charts. A front desk does not need medicine expiry alerts. That focus keeps the interface calm and the training short.

Today DigiHub ships four products under one brand: Pharmacy POS, Restaurant POS, Hotel PMS, and School Management. All share secure login, license activation, PDF/CSV reports, and MongoDB Atlas cloud data — with full isolation per store account.

We sell directly to businesses that want a clear Windows install, a fair trial, and human support during setup. No forced marketplace lock-in. No feature maze.

Mission & vision

What we stand for

Mission

Give every independent pharmacy, restaurant, hotel, and school a reliable system they can run on ordinary Windows PCs — with cloud safety and invoices that look professional.

Vision

Become the default POS choice for local businesses that want focus over feature bloat: one app, one workflow, one fair subscription.
