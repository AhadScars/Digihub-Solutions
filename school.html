About this software

DigiHub School Management helps schools and academies manage students, classes, teaching staff, fee collection, and monthly salary — on Windows with cloud MongoDB.

Admit students into classes, track parent contacts, collect tuition and other fees with receipts, and pay teacher or staff salaries from one app.

What you can do

Students & classes

Admission numbers, sections, roll, parents, and status.

Teachers & staff

Roles, subjects, employee IDs, and base salary.

Fee collection

Tuition, transport, exam fees — partial pay & receipts.

Staff salary

Monthly payroll with allowances, deductions, and vouchers.
